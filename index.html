BAGANTI’S FOUR
PRODUCT FAMILIES.

Choose the system for your project: illuminated ceilings and walls, perforated surfaces, replaceable graphic displays or architectural backlighting.

LumosCielo

Architectural luminous ceilings and surfaces

Complete illuminated ceiling, wall and feature systems. Choose Standard for square, rectangular or round panels in predefined sizes, or Custom for a system developed around your project.

Explore LumosCielo

LumosPerfora

Metal and Faux Metal systems

Perforated architectural surfaces in Metal or Faux Metal. Metal uses removable metal panels. Faux Metal uses lightweight fabric to reproduce the appearance of perforated metal. Integrated lighting is optional for both.

Explore LumosPerfora

LumosForma

Non-lit and backlit display systems

Aluminum display frames with replaceable fabric graphics. Choose Frame without lighting or Light with backlighting. SEG means silicone-edge graphic: the fabric edge fits into a groove in the frame.

Explore LumosForma

Lumosanti

Architectural backlighting

Backlighting mounted on rigid tracks and supplied in preassembled sections. Choose fixed white, adjustable white, colour-changing or individual pixel control. The layout is engineered around the surface and building services.

Explore Lumosanti
