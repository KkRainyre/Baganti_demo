Replaceable graphics with backlighting.

Aluminum frames with integrated LED backlighting and replaceable silicone-edge fabric graphics. Lighting is selected for display size, depth and brightness. Applications include Retail features, Brand environments, Trade shows, Interior signage.

Three illumination methods. One LumosForma Light system.

Select the lighting method according to required brightness, available depth, display size and surrounding light.

01

Standard Lumosanti backlighting

LEDs are arranged behind the fabric graphic to distribute light across the display.

Best suited to
General backlit displays in a range of sizes
Details to confirm
Frame depth, LED spacing, fabric light transmission and power are selected together for even illumination.
02

High-output backlighting

Higher-output LEDs provide more brightness for displays in bright surroundings. The layout is designed to reduce visible bright spots.

Best suited to
Window displays, large graphics and bright surroundings
Details to confirm
Brightness, power, heat management and dimming are confirmed for the display size and surrounding light.
03

Light-Guided Acrylic Panel

LEDs along an acrylic panel’s edges distribute light across the panel, allowing a shallower display frame.

Best suited to
Displays with limited available depth
Details to confirm
Panel size, edge lighting, fabric light transmission and maintenance access are confirmed together.
