LumosForma Frame.
Non-Lit SEG.

An aluminum frame for replaceable fabric graphics without integrated lighting. A silicone strip along the fabric edge fits into the frame’s groove. The frame stays while the graphic changes.

View product details

Choose the frame, format and mounting.

Choose wall-mounted, freestanding, suspended or window-fit installation according to the frame profile. The fabric graphic can be replaced without replacing the frame.

Replaceable graphics. No integrated light.

Aluminum frames for replaceable silicone-edge fabric graphics, without integrated lighting. The frame stays while the graphic changes. Applications include Wall graphics, Freestanding displays, Retail campaigns, Exhibitions.
