Lumosanti is Baganti’s backlighting system, mounted on rigid tracks and supplied in preassembled sections. The lighting layout is engineered for the surface, including clearances for sprinklers, ventilation and other building services.

Explore lighting capabilities
Lumosanti provides backlighting for Baganti systems.

It is used in LumosCielo, illuminated LumosPerfora and LumosForma Light. Light output, colour, spacing, controls and access are coordinated for each configuration.

One lighting platform.
Four levels of control.

Lumosanti Pure— Static White

Fixed white light. Select the colour temperature and light output for the project.

Lumosanti Tune— Dual White

Adjustable white light from 2700K (warm) to 5700K (cool).

Lumosanti Colour— RGBW

Red, green and blue light plus a separate white channel (RGBW), for colour effects and white illumination.

Lumosanti Pixel— Individual Pixel Control

Control individual light points to create programmed movement, colour transitions and effects.
