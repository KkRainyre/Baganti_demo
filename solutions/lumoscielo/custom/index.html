LumosCielo Custom.
Make the vision visible.

The project defines the form. LumosCielo Custom combines an aluminum frame, LumosFlex stretch diffuser and Lumosanti backlighting. Dimensions, mounting, finish and lighting are engineered for the project.

From imagination to realization.

Your idea is the starting point. LumosCielo Custom brings form, scale and light together around your design. These examples show just a few of the possibilities.

Organic forms

Freeform luminous panels bring soft curves and even light to a retail interior.

Floating compositions

Circular panels at different heights create a luminous composition above a stairway.

Integrated ceilings

A continuous luminous surface follows the curve of the architecture.

Linear forms

Long, narrow panels extend light across a room, with length and width developed for the project.

Mounting options include surface-mounted, suspended and recessed configurations, coordinated with the design.

Choose the required performance.

Choose Spec or Pro according to the required construction and light quality. Confirm performance and warranty terms for the selected configuration.

Spec

Custom construction with form, light output and finishes selected for the project.

  • Light output selected for the project
  • Colour rendering index (CRI): 90+
  • Wider choice of frame profiles
  • Custom colours from the RAL colour system
  • Colour (RGBW) and individual pixel control
  • Five-year warranty; terms apply

Pro

Custom construction with enhanced circuit boards, protective coating and higher colour rendering.

  • 2 mm circuit board with nickel/gold (ENIG) finish
  • Two-layer protective coating
  • CRI 95; 2-step LED colour consistency
  • Colour (RGBW) and individual pixel control
  • Ten-year warranty; terms apply
