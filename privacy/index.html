How Baganti handles information submitted through its website and direct project communications.

Information you provide

The contact form sends your request to Baganti by email through our email service provider. When you contact Baganti, you may share your name, company, email, phone number and project details. Baganti uses this information to answer your inquiry and support the project or service you request.

Website information

The website may use essential technical data required to operate securely and understand general site performance. Baganti does not sell personal information.

Cookies and location

The site keeps your language and cookie choices in your browser. On your first visit, it looks up your approximate region from your IP address through GeoJS, to show French in Quebec and English elsewhere; the result stays in your browser.

With your permission, Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits and pages viewed. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Sharing and retention

Baganti may share information with service providers or project partners when reasonably needed to operate the website, answer your request or deliver the work. Information is kept for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or to meet legal requirements.

Your choices

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, email info@baganti.com.

Updates

This notice may change when website practices or legal requirements change. The current notice is displayed on this page.
