+

What is the BAGANTI process?

+

Projects generally follow five core stages. Installation is an optional additional service:

  1. Discover — Review the application, drawings and requirements.
  2. Develop — Develop the design and select materials and product options.
  3. Engineer — Confirm structure, materials, lighting, controls and mounting.
  4. Produce — Fabricate, print, assemble, finish and check the system.
  5. Deliver — Prepare the completed system for delivery and installation.
  6. Install — Add separately quoted on-site installation, where available.

The scope and schedule are confirmed for each project.

LumosPerfora

05 questions

What is LumosPerfora?

+

LumosPerfora is BAGANTI’s family of perforated architectural systems. It is available as LumosPerfora Metal and LumosPerfora Faux Metal, with integrated lighting available when required by the application.

What is LumosPerfora Metal?

+

LumosPerfora Metal uses perforated metal panels on a magnetic support structure. Panels can be removed without tools to access components behind the surface. Collection patterns, custom patterns and optional backlighting are available.

What is LumosPerfora Faux Metal?

+

LumosPerfora Faux Metal is Baganti’s in-house-developed perforated fabric alternative to metal. It reproduces the appearance of perforated metal in widths up to 10 feet. Potential material and installation savings depend on the project.

Can LumosPerfora Faux Metal reproduce a custom pattern?

+

Yes. You can submit any pattern, image or graphic for review. You are not limited to the collection patterns.

We review pattern scale, viewing distance, material and production requirements before confirming the design.

Can LumosPerfora include lighting?

+

Yes. Both Metal and Faux Metal offer optional integrated lighting. The lighting layout, output and controls are confirmed for the selected configuration.

LumosForma

03 questions

What is LumosForma?

+

LumosForma uses silicone-edge graphics (SEG): fabric with a silicone edge that fits into a groove in an aluminum frame. The graphic can be replaced without replacing the frame.

  • LumosForma Frame — Non-Lit SEG
  • LumosForma Light — Backlit SEG

What is the difference between LumosForma Frame and LumosForma Light?

+

LumosForma Frame presents replaceable fabric graphics without integrated lighting.

LumosForma Light adds backlighting behind the replaceable fabric graphic.

What lighting methods are available for LumosForma Light?

+

LumosForma Light offers three backlighting methods, selected for the application:

  • Standard Backlighting — LEDs arranged behind the fabric to distribute light across the display.
  • High Output — Higher brightness for displays in bright surroundings or windows.
  • Light-Guided Acrylic Panel — LEDs along an acrylic panel’s edges distribute light across a shallower display.

Confirm the method according to display dimensions, frame depth, surrounding light and required brightness.

Lumosanti

09 questions

What is Lumosanti?

+

Lumosanti is BAGANTI’s architectural backlighting system mounted on rigid tracks. Choose fixed white, adjustable warm-to-cool white, red/green/blue/white colour (RGBW) or control of individual light points.

How is Lumosanti different from conventional backlighting systems?

+

A key difference is its rigid, track-based construction.

The rigid tracks support the light sources at the positions defined by the lighting layout. LED spacing and distance from the illuminated surface are coordinated during engineering.

The complete surface system can also include protective layers to help limit dust and debris. Protection and maintenance requirements depend on the selected construction and installation conditions.

Why is a rigid lighting structure important?

+

Even illumination depends on LED spacing and the distance between the LEDs and the illuminated surface.

Lumosanti’s rigid tracks support the light sources in their planned positions. The layout and surface distance are confirmed for each system.

Is Lumosanti designed to reduce future maintenance?

+

Maintenance access is considered during engineering.

Rigid tracks support the light sources, and component layout and protective layers are coordinated with the complete surface system.

Maintenance may still be required. Confirm access, operating conditions and service requirements for the selected installation.

What Lumosanti lighting options are available?

+
  • Lumosanti Pure — Fixed white light
  • Lumosanti Tune — Adjustable white light, 2700K–5700K
  • Lumosanti Colour — Red, green, blue and white light
  • Lumosanti Pixel — Control of individual light points

What is the advantage of Lumosanti RGBW?

+

RGBW means red, green, blue and white. It includes a dedicated white light channel.

The separate white LEDs provide white illumination without mixing red, green and blue. This allows the same system to provide colour effects and white light.

What does RGBW provide?

+

RGBW provides red, green and blue colour channels plus a separate white LED channel. The controls can be configured for colour effects and white illumination.

Can Lumosanti accommodate architectural obstacles?

+

The layout can be engineered around sprinklers, ventilation equipment and structural elements. Share the site drawings and required clearances so our team can review the lighting layout.

Is Lumosanti assembled before arriving on site?

+

Lumosanti can be supplied in preassembled sections. Section sizes, connections and remaining site assembly are confirmed for the project.
