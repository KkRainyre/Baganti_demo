LUMINOUS SURFACES, PERFORATED SYSTEMS,
DISPLAY SYSTEMS AND ARCHITECTURAL LIGHTING.

LumosCielo creates illuminated ceilings and surfaces. LumosPerfora creates perforated features in metal or fabric. LumosForma presents replaceable fabric graphics, with or without backlighting. Lumosanti provides the architectural backlighting.

Choose the product family.

Luminous ceilings, walls and feature surfaces.

LumosCielo combines an aluminum frame, stretch diffuser and backlighting in one system. Choose Standard for predefined forms and sizes, or Custom for project-specific form, dimensions and lighting.

Explore LumosCielo

Perforated architectural features.

Metal uses removable perforated metal panels. Faux Metal is Baganti’s in-house-developed fabric alternative, reproducing the appearance of perforated metal with less material weight. Both offer custom patterns and optional integrated lighting. Cost savings depend on the project.

Explore LumosPerfora

Changeable graphics, with or without light.

LumosForma presents replaceable fabric graphics in aluminum frames. The silicone fabric edge fits into a frame groove: this is a silicone-edge graphic (SEG). Frame has no integrated lighting. Light illuminates the graphic from behind.

Explore LumosForma

Controlled architectural backlighting.

Lumosanti is Baganti’s backlighting system mounted on rigid tracks. Choose Pure for fixed white light, Tune for adjustable warm-to-cool white, Colour for red, green, blue and white (RGBW), or Pixel to control individual light points.

Explore Lumosanti
