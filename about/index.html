A RETURN
TO THE WORK.

How Baganti brought design, engineering and manufacturing together.

Founded in Montreal in 2015, Baganti was created to bring creative development, engineering and production together under one team.

After nearly a decade in the industry, Baganti's founder stepped away for four years. An invitation from a competitor brought him back. He returned with a clear goal: create a company that could coordinate creative development, engineering, fabrication and production through one team.

During his time away, he worked in Cameroon and formed a connection with the Bamiléké community and Baganté. That experience stayed with him after his return to Montreal and inspired the name Baganti.

Baganti connects the people developing the design with the people engineering and manufacturing it. Design intent, technical requirements and production decisions are reviewed together.

Today, Baganti develops luminous ceilings and surfaces, perforated systems, fabric graphic displays and architectural backlighting. Projects can begin with an idea, drawings or a specification. Our team reviews the requirements and develops a system that can be manufactured and installed.
